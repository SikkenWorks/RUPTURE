# RUPTURE V2 — SUB OSC 2 / NORMAL CLOSE-UP

A normal cinematic close-up of the same frightened adult man, with his entire head, neck and shoulders visible in the full-size frames. This **replaces the former extreme macro close-up** for SUB OSC 2.

## Assets

- `RUPTURE-SUBOSC2-NORMAL-SPRITESHEET-224x30.png`: native 896 × 120 sheet, four columns and four rows, 224 × 30 per frame.
- `frames-224x30/`: sixteen individual native 1-bit PNG frames.
- `RUPTURE-SUBOSC2-NORMAL-HQ-SPRITESHEET-240.png`: 960 × 960 sheet, 240 × 240 per frame.
- `frames-240/`: sixteen full portrait frames.
- `RUPTURE-SUBOSC2-NORMAL-HIRES-MASTER.png`: high-resolution original sprite artwork.
- `RUPTURE-SUBOSC2-NORMAL-HQ-PREVIEW.gif`: portrait animation preview.
- `RUPTURE-SUBOSC2-NORMAL-NATIVE-PREVIEW.gif`: enlarged compact 224 × 30 animation preview.

At the 224 × 30 height, the visible image is necessarily a horizontal band across the head/eyes. The full 240 × 240 frames show the intended normal close-up with the shoulders. Cropping tracks the eyes smoothly to prevent them being clipped.

## Suggested playback

- Idle: frame 01.
- Note-on: play frames 02–12 once as the man turns toward camera.
- Held note: loop frames 13–16 for the subtle terrified stare.
- Note-off: optionally reverse to frame 01.

All compact PNGs are black-and-white 1-bit, 224 × 30 exactly. Animation display only; no firmware or sound-engine changes in this package.
